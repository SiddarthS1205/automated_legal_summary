# Demo Guide - Legal Document Summarization System

## Quick Start Demo Script

### 1. Login (30 seconds)
**What to say:** "This is our secure legal document analysis platform. Let me log in..."

**Actions:**
- Open `http://localhost:8000`
- Enter any email (e.g., `lawyer@lawfirm.com`)
- Enter any password
- Click "Login"

**What they see:** Professional blue/white login page with smooth animation

---

### 2. Dashboard Overview (30 seconds)
**What to say:** "Here's the dashboard showing our document processing activity..."

**Point out:**
- ✅ Sidebar navigation (clean, professional)
- ✅ Welcome message with user name
- ✅ Statistics cards with animated numbers:
  - Total Documents
  - Processed
  - Flagged Risks
- ✅ Quick action cards

**What they see:** Modern SaaS dashboard with professional design

---

### 3. Upload & Summary Demo (2 minutes)
**What to say:** "Let me show you how our AI analyzes legal contracts..."

**Actions:**
1. Click "Upload Contract"
2. Drag and drop any PDF or DOCX file
   - **Point out:** Visual feedback when dragging
3. Click "Generate Summary"
   - **Point out:** Multi-stage AI processing animation

**What to say during processing:** "Watch as our AI processes the document through multiple stages..."

**AI Processing Stages (3.1 seconds):**
1. 📄 Reading document structure...
2. 🔍 Extracting key clauses...
3. ⚠️ Detecting risk deviations...
4. 📊 Generating summary report...
5. ✨ Finalizing analysis...

**Point out:**
- Each stage activates sequentially with visual feedback
- Progress bar shows completion percentage
- Checkmarks appear as stages complete
- Professional spinner indicates active processing

**What they see - Summary Page:**

#### Section 1: Executive Summary
**What to say:** "Our AI generates a comprehensive one-page summary..."
- Shows intelligent paragraph summarizing the contract
- Mentions key terms, risks, and important clauses

#### Section 2: Extracted Clauses (Card View)
**What to say:** "The system automatically extracts and categorizes key clauses..."
- 8 professional cards showing:
  - Payment Terms
  - Liability and Indemnification
  - Termination Rights
  - Intellectual Property
  - Confidentiality
  - Non-Compete
  - Renewal Terms
  - Dispute Resolution
- Each card has title, full text, and category tag
- Hover effect shows interactivity

#### Section 3: Risk Flags
**What to say:** "Most importantly, our AI identifies potential risks..."
- 6 risk flags with color coding:
  - 🔴 **Red (High Risk):** Unlimited Liability, Unilateral Termination, Broad Non-Compete
  - 🟡 **Yellow (Medium Risk):** Price Escalation, IP Assignment, Mandatory Arbitration
- Each risk shows:
  - Warning icon
  - Risk title
  - Detailed description
  - Actionable recommendation

**Download Feature:**
- Click "Download Summary" button
- Shows file download with all analysis

---

### 4. Document Comparison Demo (2 minutes)
**What to say:** "Now let me show you our advanced comparison feature..."

**Actions:**
1. Click "Back to Dashboard"
2. Click "Compare Documents"
3. Upload two files (any PDF/DOCX)
   - Original version in left box
   - Revised version in right box
4. Click "Compare Documents"

**What to say during processing:** "Our AI is now comparing both documents across multiple dimensions..."

**AI Comparison Stages (3.2 seconds):**
1. 📄 Analyzing document structures...
2. 🔍 Identifying clause differences...
3. ⚖️ Detecting conflicts...
4. ⚠️ Assessing risk levels...
5. 📊 Generating comparison report...

**Point out:**
- Sequential stage activation shows sophisticated analysis
- Progress bar provides real-time feedback
- Each stage represents a different AI analysis layer
- Professional presentation builds confidence

**What they see - Comparison Page:**

#### Sticky Summary Bar (Top - Always Visible)
**What to say:** "Notice this summary bar stays visible as you scroll..."
- **Total Changes: 10**
- **High Risk: 3** (in red)
- **Conflicts: 3** (in yellow)

#### Color Legend (Top Right)
**What to say:** "The legend helps you understand our risk assessment..."
- Risk Levels: 🔴 High 🟠 Medium 🟢 Low
- Change Types: 🟡 Modified 🟢 Added 🔴 Removed

#### Advanced Filter System
**What to say:** "You can filter to focus on what matters most..."

**Demo each filter:**
1. **All Changes** - Shows all 10 differences
2. **High Risk Only** - Shows 3 critical changes
   - Liability Cap: Limited → Unlimited
   - Termination: 90 days → 30 days
   - Non-Compete: 12 months → 18 months
3. **Conflicts Only** - Shows 3 conflicting clauses
4. **Modified Only** - Shows modified clauses
5. **Added Only** - Shows new clauses
6. **Removed Only** - Shows deleted clauses

**Point out:** "Showing X of Y changes" updates in real-time

#### Comparison Table
**What to say:** "The table shows side-by-side comparison with risk assessment..."

**Highlight key rows:**
1. **Payment Terms** (Medium Risk)
   - Original: $45,000 / 30 days
   - Revised: $50,000 / 15 days
   - Shows price increase and shorter payment window

2. **Liability Cap** (🔴 High Risk - Red Background)
   - Original: Limited to 12 months fees
   - Revised: Unlimited liability
   - **Point out:** Red background indicates conflict

3. **Termination Notice** (🔴 High Risk - Red Background)
   - Original: 90 days for both parties
   - Revised: 30 days for client, 60 days for provider
   - Shows imbalanced terms

**Scroll down:**
- **Point out:** Sticky bar stays at top
- Shows all 10 changes with clear formatting
- Risk badges color-coded
- Change type indicators

- [ ] Console is closed (F12)

- [ ] Show login page
- [ ] Show dashboard with stats
